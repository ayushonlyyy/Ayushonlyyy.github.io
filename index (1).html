About

Curious first, builder always.

I'm Ayush, a student from Bihar who got pulled toward the sky early and never really looked back. Most of what I do starts with a question I can't leave alone.

I research independently, photograph the night sky, edit videos, write stories, and run Europa Crest and Cosmora. Different tools, same habit: look closely, then make something.

Right now I'm preparing for the IISER Aptitude Test (IAT) across 2026–2028, aiming to turn this curiosity into proper science.
