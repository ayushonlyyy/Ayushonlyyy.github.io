The Core Claim

The Information Dimension Hypothesis (IDH) proposes that the Internet is not merely a network of machines but constitutes a genuine emergent dimension of reality — a new layer of existence that interacts with, influences, and may be inseparable from physical spacetime itself.

Theoretical Foundations

IDH draws its conceptual roots from John Archibald Wheeler's 'It from Bit' — the idea that every particle, field, and force derives its existence from information-theoretic answers. If reality is fundamentally informational, the Internet represents the first human-constructed substrate capable of participating in this same ontological fabric.

Connection to Relativity

Einstein's Special and General Relativity demonstrated that space and time are not fixed backdrops but dynamic, interconnected dimensions. IDH asks: if spacetime is curved by mass-energy, could a sufficiently dense and interconnected information network exhibit analogous dimensional properties — bending causality, compressing epistemic distance, and restructuring how events propagate?

The Holographic Principle

The Holographic Principle, arising from black hole thermodynamics and string theory, suggests that all information in a 3D volume can be encoded on its 2D boundary. IDH extends this intuition: the Internet, as a 2D topological surface spanning the globe, may encode, reflect, and co-constitute the informational content of physical reality.

Shannon's Information Theory

Shannon's formalism treats information as a measurable, physical quantity — entropy in disguise. IDH leverages this to argue that the Internet's exponentially growing information entropy is not epistemically neutral; it may have ontological weight, making it a candidate for a new dimensional axis alongside space and time.

Implications

If IDH is correct, consciousness, culture, and computation are not peripheral to physics but central to it. The Internet becomes the fifth dimension — not in the Kaluza-Klein sense of a curled geometric dimension, but as an emergent, informational substrate that conditions reality as profoundly as the four dimensions we already know.
